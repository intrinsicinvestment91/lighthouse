Roadmap

Where Lighthouse is going, organized by architecture phase. Canonical direction: docs/ROADMAP.md. Current status: docs/ROADMAP_STATUS.md. History: docs/PACKETS.md.

Current Status

YELLOW — Operational with known gaps. Current phase: D — Repository Intelligence. The pipeline runs and produces real signals; the emphasis is signal quality and intelligence depth, not collector volume.
A profile-driven platform, not a single-mission app (ADR-0020). Public Scientific Lighthouse, Private Invention Discovery, Patent and Research Tracking, Scientific Preservation, Repository Capability Discovery, Aerospace/Defense Monitoring, Fusion Research Monitoring and custom operator profiles are first-class, co-equal missions. The shared platform stays profile-neutral (observation, normalization, facts, provenance, temporal history, candidate/hypothesis/finding mechanics, counterevidence, review history, backup, query surfaces); profiles own the interpretation (source classes, collectors, detector rules, evidence and counterevidence requirements, privacy, publishing, retention).
Next: runtime policy resolver, then lifecycle implementation. The fact layer and candidate/review loop are complete (LHP-INTEL-03A–04C). The controlled profile-switch lifecycle is designed (ADR-0024; LHP-LIFE-DESIGN-01 plus C2, C3, C4, and C5 docs-only design corrections done) but not implemented. Controlled profile switching remains design-only. Next: LHP-LIFE-RESOLVE-01 (CORE-RESOLVE-01). Parallel gap analysis: LHP-SCPRES-GAP-01 (Scientific Preservation activation readiness). LHP-LIFE-TRUTH-01 (commit 8399764) corrected stale CLI claims; switching and deactivation remain unimplemented. Facts first. Interpretation second. Findings only after evidence and review.
v1.0.0 shipped scope — RCD operator foundation only. Release v1.0.0 claims backup-gated initial activation of repository_capability_discovery v2, the observe/detect/review loop, and OSR export — not full multi-profile operation, profile switching, Gateway authority, or sovereign production completeness. Switch lifecycle design: ADR-0024 (not implemented). See V1_OPERATOR_WORKFLOW.md.

Backlog & Next Work

These are candidate items, not committed work — the full backlog lives in docs/FUTURE_PLAN.md.

BitAgent Payment-Gated Queries

After 2+ weeks of stable live data on Start9, a BitAgent payment wrapper could gate MCP intelligence queries (graph lookups, correlation explanations) behind a micropayment, consuming the bounded read-only Local Gateway. Deferred, not scheduled.

Signal Quality Tuning

Calibrate scoring weights using live Start9 data before any new collector expansion — better discrimination, fewer false positives.

DoE Archive Feed & USPTO ODP Patent Adapter

Both are blocked on external dependencies: a stable DoE newsroom RSS endpoint, and a confirmed USPTO Open Data Portal search endpoint/schema (to flip the patent provider default from disabled to odp).

Explicit Non-Goals (Near Term)

  • Broad collector sprawl — no new APIs without an interpretation plan
  • Random feature additions unrelated to graph/temporal/callsign leverage
  • Lowering Nostr or correlation gates to increase output volume
  • LLM in the deterministic core (narrative module is the isolated exception)
  • Public MCP exposure without authentication
The guiding principle Improve the meaning and depth of existing signals before adding new sources. Lighthouse's strongest near-term leverage is interpretation, not volume.
