Architecture Decision Records

The log of significant architectural decisions and why they were made. Canonical source: docs/adr/. Each record carries ID, Status, Date, Context, Decision, Consequences, repository evidence, related packets, and superseded-by.

ADRs are immutable once accepted. A decision is reversed by adding a new ADR and marking the old one superseded — never by editing it.

ADR-0020 — the significance boundary. Facts first, interpretation second, findings only after evidence and review. A repeated fact is not automatically significant; a candidate is not a hypothesis; a hypothesis is not a finding; a finding is not permission to publish or execute. Detector rules are profile-owned; the substrate stays profile-neutral. The first candidate/review loop has now shipped (ADR-0021) and it still says nothing about significance: no hypothesis layer and no finding layer exist, and a candidate carries no score, no rank and no aggregate confidence. Two earlier ADRs carry corrective notes rather than rewrites: ADR-0005's premise that the invention engine lived in the Gateway was wrong (it was in no repository at all), and ADR-0008's alert/correlation/graph pipeline for source packs is superseded in part by the fact-first pipeline.

Several ADRs are "Accepted — implementation deferred": the decision is settled, the code is future work. See each record for its exact status and evidence.
